Who We Are

A Family Business With Big Ambitions

Store To Move started as a family venture with a single goal: to give people a trustworthy place to put their things while life happens. Moving house, renovating, downsizing, or growing your business — we've seen it all and we understand the stress.

Our facility is built to the highest standards of security and cleanliness, and our team treats your belongings as if they were our own. We're proud to serve our local community and we're growing every day.
